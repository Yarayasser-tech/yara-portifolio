More than just writing code.

I started my journey with a simple question: how does an idea become something people can actually use?

Studying Business Information Systems introduced me to the connection between technology and real-world problems. As I started learning Flutter and Dart, I discovered that building an application isn't only about making the code work. It's about understanding why the product exists, who will use it, and how the experience can be better.

That perspective became an important part of how I develop. My business background helps me look at the problem behind an idea, while Flutter gives me the tools to turn that idea into a real product.

My experience as an HR Head in Enactus strengthened my communication, organization, teamwork, and leadership skills.

Today, I enjoy creating mobile applications, experimenting with interfaces, and turning ideas into practical digital experiences. From Flutter shopping applications to mobile games and business systems, every project gives me another opportunity to learn and improve.

I don't just build what I'm asked to build. I try to understand the problem, the people, and the product behind it.
